Welcome to the Smart Irrigation Portal

What We Do

This portal is built for use case KJS-AGR-01 which deals with irrigation advisory for sugarcane crops using AI and sensor-based technology. The project is carried out in collaboration with KIAAR, Sameerwadi and Godavari Biorefineries Ltd.

Sugarcane farming needs a lot of water and right now most farmers decide irrigation based on their own experience. This system tries to give them proper data-driven advice by collecting soil moisture readings, weather data and crop stage information. Last updated: 08 July 2026.

How It Helps

  • Drip schedules matched to soil moisture readings.
  • Rain-aware delays so no water is wasted.
  • Simple advice every morning before field work.
