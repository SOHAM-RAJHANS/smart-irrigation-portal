Irrigation Workflow

  1. Collect field data using sensors and farm records.
  2. Measure soil moisture, temperature, humidity and rainfall.
  3. Check weather forecast for expected rainfall.
  4. Analyze crop stage such as germination, tillering, grand growth or maturity.
  5. Generate irrigation recommendation for each plot.
  6. Send advisory to farmer through web portal or mobile message.

Crop Stages of Sugarcane

Germination
Buds start to sprout and initial root development happens. Good moisture at this stage is important for uniform germination across the field.
Tillering
New shoots or tillers come up from the base. Water management during this phase directly affects the number of millable canes later.
Grand Growth
Cane elongation is at its peak. The crop needs adequate and timely irrigation otherwise it affects the final cane height and weight.
Maturity
Cane starts accumulating sugar. Water is reduced deliberately to improve the sugar recovery rate before crushing season.
